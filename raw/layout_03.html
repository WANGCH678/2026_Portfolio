Identity

The Silent Witness

Attributed to the Dutch School
Active in Haarlem, c. 1640–1660

Details & Dimensions
Medium Oil on canvas
Dimensions 110.5 × 85.2 cm
Date c. 1642
Location Room 04, North Wall
Provenance

Formerly in the collection of the Count de Vaudreuil, Paris; his sale, December 1784. Acquired by the Marquis de Montesquiou-Fezensac. By descent through the family until 1922. Private collection, Amsterdam.

Scholarly Note

The work demonstrates the characteristic chiaroscuro of the mid-17th century Dutch tradition, with a particular focus on the psychological depth of the sitter, whose identity remains a subject of academic debate. Recent restoration has revealed the original luminosity of the background landscape.
